## The 12 patterns the leaders share

### 1. The hero shows real work, says where you are, and has one clear ask
- **Wrapmate:** a full-bleed photo of a wrapped van, a 4-word H1 ("Vehicle Wraps Made Easy") and two buttons: *Create a concept design* and *Get a quote*.
- **CWD:** a video of an installer at work, the H1 "Top car wraps & protection **in North Miami**", one red CTA, and *Get Quote* plus the phone number always in the header.
- **Purple Flare:** H1 + *Get My Free Quote* + *Call (702)…*.
- **Wrap Bullys:** a close-up video of a squeegee laying vinyl, with service tabs (Color Change / Matte PPF / Commercial).

**Pattern:** a real photo or video of the shop's own work (no stock images), the city in the headline, one primary CTA (quote) and one secondary (call/text).

### 2. You can contact them from any scroll position
- **Wrap Bullys:** a sticky **Call · Text · Quote** bar (using `tel:` and `sms:` links).
- **Tampa Auto Wraps:** "**Text us ANY hour** for your custom quote", plus a vanity number (813-27-WRAPS).
- **CWD:** a chat bubble ("Have a question? Text us here"), a sticky phone number and a red *Get Quote* button.
- **Ziebart:** separate **Request Estimate** and **Request Appointment** buttons, plus "My Local Ziebart" with today's hours and *Get Directions*.

**Pattern:** texting is the main way wrap customers make contact. Every leader makes a text/SMS link easy to reach on mobile.

### 3. A short quote form that asks for the vehicle first
- **Purple Flare:** an inline form on the homepage with service, **file upload** (photos of the car) and a promise: "We come back with a **mockup and a real number — usually the same day**."
- **Tinterz:** name, phone, email, location, service, **vehicle year/make/model**, and an SMS consent checkbox.
- **Wrapmate:** a multi-step quote that remembers progress ("**Resume your quote**" banner).

**Pattern:** ask for the vehicle, the service and photos, and **state the response time**.

### 4. Social proof with numbers, high on the page
- **Purple Flare:** "★ 4.8 from 485+ Google reviews" right under the hero, next to trust bullets (in-house design/print/install · 3M Pro Series · 1-yr warranty).
- **CWD:** a Google 4.9★ (373) widget, a *Write a Review* link, a "Top 3 2026" award badge, and **video testimonials** (including a celebrity client) with an "**I want the same →**" CTA.
- **Wrap Bullys:** 44 reviews in a slider, each tagged **Yelp/Google + date**, with **owner replies**.
- **Wrapmate:** a logo wall ("Trusted by over 10,000 brands"), review cards with **project photo + customer name + city**, and YouTube customer stories.

**Pattern:** the star rating, review count and source go near the top. The best reviews name the **vehicle and the service**.

### 5. The portfolio is the product
- **Wrap Bullys:** "Recent Work" with a **Grid/Carousel toggle**. Each card is titled **"Vehicle | Film brand + color"** (e.g. *Tesla Model Y Juniper Wrap | 3M Gloss Lucid Copper*), tagged by category, and links to its **own project page**.
- **Purple Flare:** recent-work captions like "Satin gold Audi R8" and "Colour shift Jeep".
- **Wrapmate:** gallery cards tagged by **city**, each linking to a location page, plus a **before/after slider**.
- **Tampa Auto Wraps:** portfolio filtered by category (Solid / Commercial / Truck), but captions are generic.

**Pattern:** every project gets its own page with the vehicle, the exact film (brand, finish, color) and the service. This builds trust (buyers search for "3M satin black Tesla") and brings in long-tail SEO traffic.

### 6. Site structure organized three ways: by service, by vehicle make, by area
- **Services:** color change, partial (roof, hood, chrome delete), commercial/fleet, clear PPF, color PPF, window tint, ceramic, wrap removal.
- **Makes:** Wrap Bullys (Tesla, Cybertruck, Rivian, BMW, Mercedes, Range Rover) and CWD (16 makes).
- **Areas:** CWD (Coral Gables, Brickell, Doral…), Purple Flare (Henderson, Summerlin, N. Las Vegas…) and Wrapmate (city pages).

**Pattern:** a **mega-menu** with service, make and location pages. Each page is a local SEO landing page.

### 7. They name the film brand and the warranty
All seven name their materials: 3M, Avery, XPEL, SunTek, Llumar. They state a warranty ("5-year warranty" at CWD/Wrapmate, "lifetime" at Tinterz, "1-year workmanship" at Purple Flare) and use the word certified ("3M Pro Series", "XPEL certified").

**Pattern:** the film brand plus the warranty length is what answers "will this last?"

### 8. Price anchors
- **CWD:** services shown with **"from $" prices** (PPF from $1,500 · Color change from $2,500 · Commercial from $1,900 · Tint from $250 · Ceramic from $600), plus dedicated **cost-guide pages** ("PPF 2026 Pricing Guide", "Window Tinting Cost in Miami").
- **Tinterz:** a FAQ answering "How much does it cost to wrap a car? **$2,500–$5,000**."
- **Wrapmate:** "Instant, all-inclusive pricing."
- **Ziebart:** **promotional financing**.

**Pattern:** a price range qualifies leads and ranks for "car wrap cost Tampa" searches.

### 9. A 3-step process with a timeline
- **Wrapmate:** Design (Day 1) → Print (Day 5) → Install (Week 2), "concept to installed in 15 days or less."
- **Purple Flare:** We Design → We Print → We Install.
- **Tinterz:** Consultation → Precision Installation → Quality Assurance & Follow-Up.

**Pattern:** 3 steps, with time frames when possible.

### 10. Human, local trust signals
- Years in business: Wrap Bullys "Since 2007", CWD "12 years", Ziebart "65+ years".
- Team: CWD's **Meet the Team** (names + roles) and its **Google 3D shop tour**; Purple Flare's "family owned".
- Location: address, **hours**, map, and nearby landmarks ("Near the 405 & 110 freeways").
- **Languages:** CWD says "Services available in **English, Spanish and Russian**."

### 11. Offers and urgency
- **Tampa Auto Wraps:** "**FREE Ceramic Coating with any Full Wrap ($799 value)** – Limited Time."
- **Ziebart:** "Get up to a $100 Visa Prepaid Card" promo, plus gift cards.

### 12. Interactive "try before you buy" (the premium differentiator)
- **CWD:** a **Wrap Visualizer**, highlighted in the main nav.
- **Wrapmate:** a free online **concept design tool**.

## What the Tampa competition does poorly (your openings)

| Gap at Tinterz / Tampa Auto Wraps | Opportunity for Hermes Wraps |
| --- | --- |
| No star rating or review count near the top; testimonials have no stars, photos or vehicles | Put a Google rating badge under the hero; tag each review with vehicle + service |
| Generic portfolio captions ("Corvette", "Sport cars") | Caption every job as **"Vehicle \| 3M film + color"** with its own page |
| Dated, cluttered layouts; Tinterz is mostly one long form | A modern, mobile-first, photo-led design |
| Prices only in an FAQ (Tinterz) or not at all | **"From $" prices** on each service card, plus a "Car Wrap Cost in Tampa" guide |
| No partial-wrap packages | Package the entry-level jobs (see below) |
| No Spanish | A bilingual site or a "Hablamos Español" badge (Tampa Bay has a large Spanish-speaking market) |
| No neighborhood pages | Pages for South Tampa, Brandon, Westchase, Wesley Chapel, St. Petersburg, Clearwater, Riverview |

## Recommendations for Hermes Wraps, by priority

### Must-have (launch)
1. **Hero:** your own best wrap photo or video, H1 *"Premium 3M Vehicle Wraps in Tampa"*, primary **Get a Free Quote**, secondary **Text Us**.
2. **Sticky mobile bar:** `Call` · `Text` · `Quote` (`tel:` / `sms:` links).
3. **Quote form, 5 fields:** name, phone, vehicle (year/make/model), service (dropdown), photo upload, plus SMS consent. Promise: *"Mockup + price within 24 hours."*
4. **Trust strip under the hero:** ★ Google rating · "3M certified film" · warranty length · "Tampa, FL". Start collecting Google reviews now; it's the one thing you can't fake later.
5. **Portfolio:** your Facebook work (e.g. *3M High Gloss Black partial wrap*) rebuilt as cards titled **"Vehicle | 3M Gloss Black – Partial Wrap"**, filterable by service.
6. **Service cards with "from $" prices:** Partial wraps, Full color change, Chrome delete, Roof/Hood, Commercial, PPF/Tint (if offered).
7. **Footer:** address, hours, map, phone, Instagram/Facebook/TikTok.

### Should-have (first 1–3 months)
8. **Partial-wrap packages,** your entry product and a fast way to win your first reviews: *Roof wrap · Hood wrap · Chrome delete · Mirror caps · Blackout package*, each with a price.
9. **3-step process:** Consult & mockup → Install (X days) → Final inspection & care guide.
10. **One page per service** plus a **"Car Wrap Cost in Tampa (2026)"** guide, and an FAQ covering Florida sun/UV, lifespan, leased cars, paint safety and aftercare.
11. **Before/after slider** for partials and chrome deletes. The contrast sells them.
12. **Video:** 15-second install clips and customer-reaction reels (copy CWD's "I want the same →" button under each).
13. **About/Team:** your face, story and shop photos. People buy from people at a new shop.

### Nice-to-have (growth)
14. **Neighborhood landing pages** and **make pages** (Tesla, Cybertruck, Jeep, F-150, BMW): this is how CWD and Wrap Bullys rank.
15. **Color/finish picker or visualizer** (start simple: pick a 3M color and see it on a silhouette).
16. **Spanish version** or a bilingual toggle.
17. **Launch offer** (e.g. "Free ceramic coating on full wraps" or "$X off your first partial") and **financing** for full wraps.
18. **Instagram feed embed** to keep the homepage fresh with no extra work.
