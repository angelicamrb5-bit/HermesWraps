## Gaps to close before targeting medium/large US companies

Corporate fleet and marketing buyers look for proof the shop can handle volume and process.
The current photos show one vehicle per client. Missing:

1. **Fleet shots:** 3+ matching vehicles lined up. This is the single most persuasive
   image for a fleet manager.
2. **Process:** design proof, printing, lamination, installation in the bay, a detail
   close-up of edges and seams. These show the 8+ years of craft.
3. **Before / after** pairs for at least 3 jobs.
4. **People:** the owner and the installation crew. Buyers hire a contractor they can see.
5. **Consistent angles** per job: front 3/4, side profile, rear 3/4, one detail.
6. **Originals** for the low-resolution files: Rey D Reyes, Life Decor, 3&9 Glass, Blay's,
   and the Hughes Electric screenshot.
7. **Client permission + one-line testimonials** from 3–5 of the commercial clients
   (BMS, Bosch, Olympus, DKDY, Dial 3 are the best fits).

## Before publishing

- Blur license plates (visible on several vans and trucks).
- Confirm each client is happy to be shown by name.
- Keep the busy, colorful wraps on light ivory/stone pages with generous spacing. Per the
  color guide, the site should frame the work, not compete with it.
