## Logo

- Winged Spartan-style helmet + angled italic wordmark "HERMES WRAPS" in gold with dark outlines, black "VINYL SPECIALISTS" bar, on a burgundy disc with a gold ring (`assets/brand/logo-reference.png`).
- Place only on Midnight Wine, Hermes Burgundy, or a dark plate. Never on busy photos, never on ivory without a dark plate.
- Minimum clear space: the height of the "VINYL SPECIALISTS" bar on all sides.
- The supplied logo is a 1024px JPG with an AI-generator sparkle in the bottom-right corner. Treat it as a reference only. For production, request a clean vector (SVG/PDF) plus transparent PNGs in full-color and one-color gold. In mockups, crop the sparkle out or use a placeholder.
- Don't recolor, stretch, add effects, or rebuild the wordmark in a font.

## What the palette says

- Burgundy = established, trustworthy (institutional, stable - not a fly-by-night shop).
- Gold = premium craftsmanship - sparing use only (≈10% of a screen) or it turns flashy.
- Midnight + Carbon = precision - the sharp lines of the emblem and the tagline bar.
- Copper + the angled wordmark = young energy - shown through movement and accents, not louder colors.
- Balance: mostly light (ivory/stone), burgundy as the confident anchor, gold as the finishing touch.

## 60 / 30 / 10

- 60% Ivory + Stone: page backgrounds and breathing room.
- 30% Burgundy + Midnight: header, hero, footer, primary buttons.
- 10% Gold: buttons on dark, icons, dividers, key numbers like "8+ years".

## Buttons and links (from the guide)

- Primary: Burgundy pill, Ivory label. Hover: Burgundy Lift.
- On dark: Wraps Gold pill, Carbon label. Hover: Gold Highlight.
- Secondary: Ivory/transparent pill, 2px Burgundy outline, Burgundy label.
- Links: Burgundy, underlined on hover. On dark: Gold Highlight.
- Focus ring: 2px Ring Gold, 2px offset, visible on every interactive element.

## Print note

CMYK values in the guide are computed starting points. For vinyl and print, match to a Pantone/vinyl swatch and proof physically.
