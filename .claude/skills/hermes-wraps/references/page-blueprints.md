## How we work (process & standards)

The most important page for design-system teams.

1. Hero: H1 "We start with your standards." Lead: their guidelines are the brief.
2. Process timeline (5-6 steps): Share standards & files → Review & site/vehicle assessment → Proof & approval → Production → Installation → QA & photo handover. Step details only as confirmed by the client.
3. "What we need from you" checklist (brand guidelines, vector files, color references, quantities, locations, deadlines) - helpful, specific.
4. "What you get from us" Spec block (proofs, approvals, QA record).
5. Quality section: detail macro gallery with captions explaining what to look for (edges, seams, alignment).
6. FAQ (process-focused).
7. Closing CTA band.
