# Voice and copy

## Voice

- **Precise** - concrete nouns, specific verbs, real numbers only. "Panels aligned across the roll-up door," not "stunning results".
- **Respectful of expertise** - write to peers who own brand standards. No basics, no hype.
- **Committed** - first person plural ("we"), personal accountability. Short, direct promises we can keep.
- **Confident, not loud** - calm statements, few exclamation marks (none in headlines).

Write the client first: "your brand", "your specs", "your fleet". Hermes Wraps second.

## Headline patterns

- Standard first: "Your brand standards, applied exactly." / "Built to your spec, panel by panel."
- Process: "We start with your standards." / "Proofed, approved, then installed."
- Proof: "Work measured against our clients' standards."
- Story: "8+ years of craft. Now ready for your next challenge."
- Close: "We focus on your quality exigencies."

Avoid: "Unleash", "elevate your brand", "world-class", "best in the business", "one-stop shop", "cutting-edge".

## The signature line

"We focus on your quality exigencies." - client wording. Use verbatim in the four fixed places (home hero, closing band, footer, consultation intro). Don't translate, paraphrase or shorten it in those spots. In body copy, express the same idea in plain language with the supporting phrasings from `SKILL.md` §3.

## CTA library

- Primary (always): **Book a free consultation**
- Compact: Book free consultation
- Secondary: See our work · How we work · Explore {service}
- Reassurance (under primary): Free · No obligation · We review your specs before quoting.
- Inline prompt: "Have a standard we should meet? Book a free consultation."

## Microcopy

- Form intro: "Tell us what this project needs to meet. We'll review it before we talk."
- Requirements hint: "Brand guidelines, color references, quantities, locations, deadlines - whatever applies."
- Upload hint: "PDF, AI, SVG or EPS. Up to `[CLIENT TO SUPPLY]` MB."
- Error: "Enter a work email so we can send your consultation details."
- Success: "Thank you - we'll review your requirements and contact you within `[CLIENT TO SUPPLY]`."
- Empty project filter: "No projects in this category yet. See all work."

## Story copy guidance

Confirmed points: 8+ years of hands-on experience; first alongside talented mentors (tutors), now independently; commitment to the client; skill to reach the highest quality standards; seeking new challenges to grow; ready to prove it performs as expected for any company that hires it.

Frame: experience earned under strong mentors + the focus and accountability of a company that's building its name on every job. Never apologize for being new; never invent team size, client names or history beyond these points.

## Placeholders

Any unconfirmed fact: `[CLIENT TO SUPPLY: what is needed]`. Keep placeholders visible in drafts; never replace them with plausible-sounding inventions.
